Beta Program

Run ThetaPrime on your own account. Get paid in time, not cash.

What you're signing up for

A Windows app, a real broker, real orders.

ThetaPrime is a desktop trading app for Windows. Beta testers install it, connect it to their own Zerodha Kite, ICICI Breeze, or Dhan account, and run it against real capital during the beta window. There is no paper-trading-only track — the point of the beta is to see how the app behaves with real orders, real fills, and real money on the line.

Incentives

Free license time for completing the window — more for finding bugs.

Complete the beta window with real usage on your account and you get 6 free months added to your license, no bug report required. On top of that, every bug you report during the window earns a bounty based on severity.

+1 month Minor

Cosmetic, a display glitch, or anything with no realistic path to affecting an order or your money.

+2 months Medium

Could plausibly cause a wrong or missed order, a confusing risk state, or bad data reaching a decision — even if it didn't this time.

+6 months Major

Could plausibly place an unintended live order, miss a stop-loss, or otherwise put your capital at risk — even if what actually happened was harmless.

Severity is judged on potential impact to money or orders, not just what happened in your one report — a near-miss that could have gone badly is scored on how badly it could have gone.

Reporting a bug

One report, one tracked issue.

Report anything unexpected from inside the app — it automatically creates a tracked GitHub issue on your behalf, so nothing you flag gets lost or has to be re-explained later. Include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. A near-miss that resolved itself on its own is still worth reporting.

Disclaimer

  • Beta software provided as-is; you are trading with your own broker account and real capital, at your own risk.
  • ThetaPrime and its operator are not liable for any trading losses, missed or incorrect orders, or other financial impact arising during the beta period.
  • Nothing in this app or program is investment advice.

Safety instructions

  • Monitor real trades closely, especially for the first few days on each new build — don't run the app unattended during market hours during the beta window.
  • Start with small size/lots before trusting a strategy at normal size.
  • Periodically verify the app's shown positions against your broker's own terminal — don't rely solely on the dashboard during beta.
  • Know how to manually square off via your broker's own terminal if the app misbehaves.
  • Don't disable stop-loss/risk guards to "test" without understanding the consequences.
  • Report anything unexpected immediately, even a near-miss that resolved itself.
